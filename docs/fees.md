# Fees and rewards

Every trade pays the market's fee, chosen at launch from 1, 2 or 3 percent. On the curve the fee is taken in the word coin. In the pool the fee is the LP fee, collected by the launchpad from its own position.

The ledger splits every fee three ways:

* 40 percent to holders of that market, paid in the word coin
* 30 percent to the buyback of the exchange coin
* 30 percent to the exchange

Holders are paid every fifteen minutes with nothing to claim. A market pays once it has at least one hundred dollars unpaid. A wallet counts when it holds at least five dollars of the token, and a payout under one dollar waits for the next cycle. The launchpad, the pool, the router and the treasury never receive rewards.
